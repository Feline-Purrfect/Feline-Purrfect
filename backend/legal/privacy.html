Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Feline Purrfect (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Feline Purrfect is operated by {{OPERATOR}}. Questions or requests: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

The short version

  • We collect what you enter so the app can work: your account, your cats’ profiles and photos, and their daily logs.
  • Ask, the AI assistant, sends your questions (and, in a chat about a cat, that cat’s profile and logs) to Anthropic, only after you allow it. You can turn this off anytime.
  • We don’t sell your information, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we never see it in readable form after sign-up.
  • Cat profiles: name, sex, birth date (or an estimate), adoption date, breed, colour, weight, microchip number if you add one, and photos you upload.
  • Daily logs: food and water amounts, litter box counts, symptoms, medications and doses, weight, temperature, teeth brushing, and any notes you write.
  • Ask chats: the questions you ask, the answers, chat titles, any answer you report and the reason you give, and the date you allowed AI data sharing.
  • Feedback: if you send us feedback or a bug report from the app or by email, whatever you include in it, such as your name and email.

Information collected automatically

  • Diagnostics: when the app crashes or hits an error, a report with technical details: device model, operating system and app version, the error, the screens leading up to it, and your IP address. For a small share of sessions, and when an error occurs, this can include a replay of how the app’s screens changed, with on-screen text and images masked.
  • Security and server logs: your IP address is used briefly to limit repeated requests and prevent abuse, and may appear in our hosting provider’s logs. Our own request logs record which feature was used and whether it worked, not your content.
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, a token that lets us send you reminders.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we don’t access your photo library except for the photos you choose to upload.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the app: storing your cats and logs, showing trends and reminders, and answering your questions in Ask. (Necessary to provide the service you asked for.)
  • To run Ask: only with your permission, which you can withdraw in Settings. (Consent.)
  • To keep the app secure and working: sign-in, preventing abuse, fixing crashes and errors, and reviewing answers you report so we can make Ask safer. (Our legitimate interest in a safe, reliable service.)
  • To contact you: verification and password reset codes, and important notices about your account or these policies.
  • To meet legal obligations, when the law requires it.

We don’t use your information for advertising, and we don’t sell or rent it.

3. Ask and AI

Ask is powered by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Nothing is sent to Anthropic until you allow it on the screen shown before your first question.

  • General chats send your question and the earlier messages in that chat.
  • Chats about a cat also send that cat’s profile and logs, usually from the last 30 days, and older days when a question needs them. Your notes are included. Photos are never sent.
  • Anthropic processes this to write the answer under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on this content. Anthropic keeps it only for a limited time under its own policies.
  • We save your chats in your account so you can come back to them. You can delete any chat at any time, and turn AI data sharing off in Settings.
  • Answers are generated automatically and can be wrong. They are not veterinary advice; see our Terms of Use.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app, under contracts that limit their use of it to providing their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Render: hosting for our servers.
  • Anthropic: AI answers in Ask, only with your permission.
  • Sentry: crash and error reports.
  • Email delivery provider: sending verification and password reset codes.
  • Expo, Apple and Google: delivering push notifications, if you allow them.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or others, or as part of a merger, sale or transfer of the app, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

5. Where your information is stored

Our servers and our service providers are located in the United States, so your information is stored and processed there, which may be outside your country. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account, cats, logs, photos and chats are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Deleting a chat, photo, log or cat removes it right away. Deleting your account removes your account and everything in it, including your photos and chats.
  • Copies may remain in our providers’ backups and diagnostic records for a limited period before they are overwritten or expire.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your information in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Withdraw consent for Ask in Settings → AI data sharing.
  • Photo and notification permissions can be changed in your device settings.
  • Request a copy of your information, or ask any other privacy question, by emailing {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We will reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as to object to or restrict certain uses, or to complain to a privacy authority (in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada). We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

8. Children

Feline Purrfect is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls on our database, and private storage for photos, which are only shown through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, we will let you know in the app before they take effect.
